The History of Dharwad

Dharwad is one of the historically important cities of North Karnataka. It has a rich heritage shaped by centuries of history, culture, literature and music.

The region of Dharwad has witnessed the rule of several important dynasties. Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Vijayanagara rulers, Adil Shahis, Marathas and the British have all influenced the region at different periods of history.

Dharwad's location made it an important stopping point between the hills of Malenadu and the plains of Bayaluseeme. Because of this geographical position, the city developed as an important centre for trade and travel.

Over the years, Dharwad became especially famous for education, literature, music and culture. It has been home to many celebrated writers, poets and musicians.

How Did Dharwad Get Its Name?

The Story Behind the Name

The name Dharwad is traditionally associated with the old name "Dwarawata".

The word "Dwara" means "door" or "gateway", while "Wata" or "Wada" refers to a settlement or town.

Dharwad was situated like a gateway between the Malenadu hills and the plains of Karnataka. It was therefore an important resting and stopping place for travellers.

Over time, the name is believed to have changed through local usage and eventually became Dharwad.
