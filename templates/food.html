Dharwad Peda

Dharwad Peda is the most famous sweet associated with the city. It is traditionally prepared using milk solids and sugar.

Dharwad's food culture is also strongly connected with the traditional cuisine of North Karnataka.

🌾 Jolada Rotti

Jolada rotti is a traditional north karnataka dish made from jowar flour.It is soft,tasty and usually enjoyed with spicy curries, chutney, or vagetables.A simple dish that reflects the rich food culture of karnataka.

🍬 Local Sweets

Dharwad is especially famous for its peda and other traditional sweet-shop treats.
