About DharwadaS Peda

Dharwada Peda is one of the most famous traditional sweets associated with Dharwada and North Karnataka. It is known for its distinctive brown colour, soft texture and caramel-like taste. The sweet has become an important part of Dharwada's food identity and is enjoyed by both local people and visitors.

History of Dharwada Peda

Dharwada Peda has a long history in the city and is traditionally associated with the Thakur family. Over generations, the sweet became closely connected with Dharwad and eventually became one of the city's best-known food specialties.

What Makes Dharwad Peda Special?

🥛 Milk

Milk is one of the main ingredients and is slowly cooked to create the characteristic texture.

🍚 Sugar

Sugar gives the peda its sweetness and contributes to its distinctive colour and taste.

✨ Traditional Method

Slow cooking and careful preparation help create the unique flavour associated with Dharwad Peda.

How Dharwad Peda is Prepared

1

Fresh milk is heated and slowly cooked until it becomes thick.

2

Sugar is added to the thickened milk mixture and it is cooked further.

3

The mixture develops its characteristic brown colour and rich flavour.

4

The prepared mixture is shaped into small peda pieces.

5

The peda is traditionally finished with powdered sugar before serving.

Where Can You Find Dharwad Peda?

Traditional Peda Shops

Dharwad has several traditional sweet shops where visitors can buy Dharwad Peda.

Line Bazaar Area

Line Bazaar is historically associated with the traditional Dharwad Peda trade.

Across Dharwad City

Dharwad Peda is also available at various sweet shops and food stores throughout the city.

Why Is Dharwad Peda Famous?

Dharwad Peda is more than just a sweet. It represents the food culture and identity of Dharwad. Its distinctive taste and traditional preparation have made it a popular food souvenir for people visiting the city.
