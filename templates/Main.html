North Karnataka.

Dharwada is a historic spot in the state of karnataka and an a important cultural and educational centre of north karnataka. It has a rich Heritage shaoed by a several dynastis, includig,chalukyas,rastrakutas, vijayanagara rulers and later deccan powers.

Dharwada is known for its contributions to Kannada Literature,Hindustani Clasical Music, Education and Arts.Its Historical Landmarks,Traditional Culture and Educational Institutions have given the city a unique identity.




Forensic Science Institutions in Dharwad

National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU)

Dharwad is home to the National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), an institution dedicated to education and research in forensic sciences and allied fields. The Dharwad campus aims to strengthen forensic science education in Karnataka and neighbouring states.

Forensic Science applies scientific knowledge to investigations and the justice system. It includes areas such as forensic biology, chemistry, fingerprints, forensic psychology and digital forensics.

Courses and Academic Areas

  • Integrated B.Sc.–M.Sc. Forensic Science
  • B.Tech–M.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security specialization)
  • Professional Diploma in Crime Scene Management

🎓 IIT & IIIT Dharwada

Dharwada is becoming an important centre for technology and higher education in North Karnataka. IIT Dharwada and IIIT Dharwada add to the city's growing reputation as an educational hub.

Indian Institute of Technology Dharwada

IIT Dharwada is an Institute of National Importance established in 2016 under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It started its academic journey under the mentorship of IIT Bombay.

The institute offers programs in areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, Engineering Physics, Mathematics and Computing, and other emerging areas.

IIT Dharwada also offers postgraduate and research opportunities, including M.Tech, BS-MS and Ph.D. programs.

Visit IIT Dharwada

Indian Institute of Information Technology Dharwada

IIIT Dharwada was established in 2015 and is an Institute of National Importance. It focuses mainly on information technology, innovation, research and technology education.

The institute provides education in areas including Computer Science and Engineering, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence and Computing.

IIIT Dharwada aims to develop skilled technology professionals and encourage students to use technology to solve real-world problems.

Visit IIIT Dharwada
