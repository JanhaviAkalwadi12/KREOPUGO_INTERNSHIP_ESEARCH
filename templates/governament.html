Government Administration in Dharwad

Dharwad has several government offices responsible for district administration, rural development, revenue, policing and civic services.

These offices help citizens with administration, public services, development activities and other official matters.

📌 Administrative Structure

Dharwad's administration follows different levels of responsibility. The Deputy Commissioner heads the district, while Assistant Commissioners manage subdivisions and Tahsildars head taluks.

Revenue Inspectors work at the circle level and Village Accountants serve villages or groups of villages.
